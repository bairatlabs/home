Number Rush

Help and support

Number Rush is a math game for children aged 7 to 9. Pip is walking home, and every bridge, boulder and fork on the trail is an adding or subtracting problem within 20. It has no ads, no purchases and no accounts, and it works without the internet.

How to play

  1. Tap Play, then tell the game who started it: Me! or A grown-up.
  2. Pip walks until something blocks the path. A bridge is missing a plank: pick the number that fits. A boulder needs the answer. At a sign, pick the path that makes the sign’s number.
  3. Stuck? Tap Help for a hint. The pause button at the top left stops the game, or ends it for today.
  4. Five stages make a trail. Some stages hide a gem for the gem box on the title screen.

For grown-ups

Tap Grown-ups on the title screen and answer the multiplication question. You’ll see each session: the date, when it started, how many minutes it lasted, who started it and how far it got. Share 14-day report makes a picture of every day since the game was first opened on the device, which you can send with your device’s share sheet. Play history never leaves the device unless you share it. See the privacy policy.

Installing the test version

iPad or iPhone: install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invitation email or link we sent you on the same device and tap Install.

Android tablet or phone: open the testing link we sent you, tap to become a tester, then install Number Rush from the Google Play page it opens. Use the Google account we invited.
