Provide an email you control and keep account credentials private. Children do not create accounts or receive account messages. You are responsible for choosing a suitable parent PIN and supervising activities outside the app.

The app provides structured educational practice, parent scripts, and real-world missions. It does not provide medical care, therapy, diagnosis, or emergency services. Results vary; completing a program does not guarantee a behavioral outcome. Adapt suggestions to your child's needs, communication style, culture, and comfort. Children should not be pressured to make eye contact, speak, or comply with unsafe situations.
