Etiquette Coach for Kids: support and account deletion

Updated September 2, 2026. Support is provided by Bairat Labs, LLC.

Contact support

Email bairatlabs@gmail.com with "Etiquette Coach support" in the subject. Include the app version, device platform, and a short description of the issue. The Support ID shown in Family can help locate parent purchase access. Do not send passwords, parent PINs, verification codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary information about your child.

Delete a child profile only

  1. Enter Parent Mode and open Family → Privacy.
  2. Choose Delete child profile and confirm.
  3. Wait for completion. If an error appears, retry before assuming the data is gone.

This removes the child nickname, age band, selected goal, practice progress, saved session, mission observations, and reminder from this device. The parent account, purchase access, and parent PIN remain. No subscription is required for deletion.

Delete the parent account

  1. Sign in to your parent account and leave Kid Mode through the parent gate.
  2. Open Family → Privacy → Delete account and local data.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. Keep the device online until the request completes.

The app deletes the Cognito parent account and clears this device's child data, progress, parent PIN, and reminder. It cannot remotely erase another device's local data or existing platform backups. The app reports failures for retry. If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, email the contact above from your parent account email with "Etiquette Coach account deletion" in the subject. We may need to verify account ownership; never send a password or verification code.

RevenueCat and store transaction records are not automatically erased by deleting the Cognito account. Include any request concerning those records in your email. We assess applicable retention exceptions and respond within the periods required by law. See the Privacy Policy for the data categories and deletion limitations.

Cancel billing separately

Deleting the account or uninstalling does not stop an active monthly subscription. Cancel through Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, using the original purchasing store account. The one-time Stop Interrupting program does not renew.

Missing purchases or progress

Use Restore Purchases in Parent Mode with the store account that bought access. Avoid making another purchase to repair a missing entitlement. Child progress stays on the device in this release, so restoring a purchase does not recover deleted progress or copy progress from a different device.
