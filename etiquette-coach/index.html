Support, privacy, and your account

Short, parent-led social-skills practice for children. This is the support and legal hub for Etiquette Coach for Kids by Bairat Labs, LLC.

  • Privacy Policy: what stays on your device, which services handle parent accounts and purchases, and your controls.
  • Terms of Use: focused-program access, monthly coaching, purchases, and cancellation.
  • Support and account deletion: get help, delete local child data, or request parent-account deletion.

Email support

Contact bairatlabs@gmail.com. Please include "Etiquette Coach" in your subject and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, parent PINs, verification codes, or unnecessary information about your child.

Billing is separate from account deletion

Deleting your app account or uninstalling does not cancel a monthly subscription. Manage subscriptions in the store account that made the purchase. The one-time Stop Interrupting program has no renewal.
