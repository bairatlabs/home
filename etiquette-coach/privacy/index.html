Etiquette Coach for Kids: Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026. This policy covers Etiquette Coach for Kids and its app-specific support and legal pages.

Who operates the app

Etiquette Coach for Kids is operated by Bairat Labs, LLC, 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713, United States. Contact bairatlabs@gmail.com with the subject "Etiquette Coach privacy request".

The short version

An adult parent or legal guardian owns the account and supervises child practice. Children do not create accounts. In this version, child profiles and practice progress are stored on the device, not in our cloud database. Parent account information is handled by Amazon Cognito. Purchase access is handled by RevenueCat and the relevant app store. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app has no advertising SDK, public child profiles, messaging, or open-ended child chat.

Information used by the app

Local device data may be included in device or browser backups depending on the platform and your settings. Local profile storage is not an encrypted document vault. Protect the device and its backups with your own device security settings.

Voice, permissions, and network activity

Children can practice aloud or silently. This version does not record, transcribe, retain, or upload audio and does not request microphone access for practice. It does not send child content to an AI provider. A legacy voice preference can be cleared in Privacy controls; it does not enable recording.

Notifications and device authentication are optional, parent-initiated features. Denying them does not block the app's basic use. Internet connections to account, billing, store, and public support services expose connection information such as IP addresses to those services. No separate product-analytics or advertising service is integrated in this build. Store and operating-system diagnostics remain governed by the relevant platform settings and policies.

Service providers

  • Amazon Web Services: Cognito parent authentication. Our Cognito pool is configured in US East (Northern Virginia), us-east-1. See AWS privacy information.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation and access management, including account identifiers and purchase records. The app does not send child profiles, practice results, or parent email as RevenueCat customer attributes. See RevenueCat privacy information.
  • Apple or Google: store payment, purchase records, and subscriptions, under their respective Apple and Google policies. We do not receive your full payment-card details from the app store.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these public support and legal pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or embedded third-party fonts. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the public website and processes connection information for delivery and security. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. This website infrastructure does not receive the app's locally stored child profile or practice progress.
  • Google provides the Gmail inbox used for support. Messages and attachments you send are processed by Gmail and used by Bairat Labs to handle your request. See Google's privacy policy.

Information may also need to be disclosed to comply with applicable law or to protect security and legal rights. We do not authorize these providers to use child practice content for advertising; this version does not transmit that content to them.

Parent control and children's privacy

The app is designed for parent-led use with children approximately 5 to 12 years old. Restricted Kid Mode has structured choices, no unrestricted text entry, no external links, and no purchase controls. A parent gate is a device-access control, not by itself proof of parental consent under every applicable law.

Parents can review local profile and progress information in Family and Progress. They can clear a child profile in Family → Privacy → Delete child profile. That removes the local child profile, progress, saved practice session, and reminder while retaining the parent account and PIN. No purchase is needed to use privacy controls. To stop participation, stop launching Kid Mode and delete the child profile.

If a child has supplied personal information to us through support or another channel, contact us so we can investigate, verify the appropriate parent, and address deletion. Before adding recording, child cloud sync, or other materially different collection, we will reassess the notice and consent requirements. The parent-account checkbox in this version is not a claim of independently verified parental consent.

Deletion and retention

Family → Privacy → Delete account and local data requests deletion of the signed-in Cognito parent account and clears this device's profile, progress, parent PIN, and reminder. A connection to Cognito is needed to delete the cloud account. If deletion fails, the app displays an error so you can retry. Data on another device or in an existing device backup is not remotely removed.

Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel separately through the store account that purchased it. Purchase records may remain with RevenueCat, Apple, or Google; account deletion in this version does not automatically erase RevenueCat customer records. Contact us for a request concerning those records so we can review it and any applicable retention obligations.

Local profile records remain on your device until you delete them through the app or remove the relevant device/browser storage. The Cognito parent record remains until account deletion. Purchase records are retained separately to establish purchase access, handle restoration or disputes, and meet applicable transaction-record obligations; they are not automatically removed when the Cognito account is deleted. Support correspondence is handled according to the request, follow-up needs, and applicable legal obligations. Provider security logs and backups follow the relevant provider's retention processes and are not immediately erased by an in-app deletion action. Contact us to request deletion or information about retained records; any retention exception must be assessed for that request.

Your requests and international use

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of personal information and to complain to a privacy regulator. Contact the email above. We may reasonably verify your identity, but will not ask for your password or one-time verification code. We cannot retrieve a child profile stored only on your device. We will handle applicable requests within the periods required by law.

Bairat Labs is based in the United States, and the parent account service is configured there. Provider processing may occur in other locations, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. Contact us with questions about processing locations or safeguards applicable to your information. This policy does not limit any rights or protections that apply under your local law.

Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Material changes to collection or use require appropriate notice and, where required, a new parent choice or consent. Continued use alone is not a substitute for legally required consent.
