Garbh Sanskar: Shloka Sadhana

A daily sadhana for the nine months of pregnancy, for iPhone, in English and Hindi.

HelpPrivacy

Every week of pregnancy has its own Sanskrit shloka, learned the way shlokas have always been learned: by listening. The phone chants a phrase, pauses for you to say it back, and moves on, until you can chant the whole verse.

Each day, five short steps
  • Garbh Samvad: a few words to say aloud to your baby
  • This week's shloka, with its meaning
  • A story from the Ramayan, the Mahabharata, the saints and more
  • Japa on a 108-bead mala
  • A sankalp for the day

Around them: a tanpura made in the app, guided meditations and gentle breath practice; your baby's week, a contraction timer, appointments and a hospital-bag list; food ideas, the sanskaras of pregnancy and birth, and Sanskrit baby names; a journal; and the forty days after birth.

No account and no ads. Your details stay on your iPhone. Garbh Sanskar is a spiritual practice, not medical care.
