Garbh Sanskar: Shloka Sadhana, for iPhone.

The daily sadhana

Today shows five short steps set to your week of pregnancy: Garbh Samvad, this week's shloka, a story, japa and a sankalp. Do them in any order, at any time of day. Nothing is counted as missed; the Journey tab only counts the days you sat.

Learning the shloka

Tap Shloka, then "Learn phrase by phrase". The phone chants one phrase and pauses so you can say it back, then moves to the next. When it feels familiar, use Chant to say the whole verse 1, 3 or 11 times with the words highlighted.

A clearer voice

The chanting uses your iPhone's Hindi voice. For the clearest sound, download an enhanced Hindi voice: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content (called Read & Speak on newer iOS) → Voices → Hindi, then choose a voice marked Enhanced or Premium.

The tanpura and meditations

They keep playing when the screen locks. Use the moon button on the tanpura to set a sleep timer that fades it out.

Your details

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone; there is no account. Moving to a new iPhone, restore from your backup and your practice comes with it. To erase everything, use Settings → Delete all my data in the app. Read the privacy policy.

Health

Garbh Sanskar is a spiritual practice and doesn't give medical advice. Please talk to your doctor or midwife about anything to do with your health or your baby's. If something feels wrong, such as bleeding, fluid leaking, a bad headache, or your baby moving less, call your maternity unit or emergency number straight away.
