Privacy Policy

Garbh Sanskar: Shloka Sadhana, for iPhone. Effective 7 November 2026, updated 12 November 2026.

In short: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter in the app stays on your iPhone. We never receive it. The only outside service the app uses is RevenueCat, to confirm purchases, and it sees an anonymous ID and your purchase history, not your details.

This policy is from Bairat Labs, LLC ("we"), which makes the app. It covers the app and this website's Garbh Sanskar pages.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

To make the practice personal, the app saves what you tell it:

  • your name, your baby's pet name and your partner's name, if you give them;
  • your due date (or the date your last period began, or your week), and your baby's birth date if you tap "Baby has arrived";
  • your language, your chosen mantra and reminder time, and other settings;
  • your practice: steps done each day, japa counts, shlokas chanted and stories told;
  • what you record in the care tools: contractions, baby's movements, appointments and the hospital-bag list;
  • your journal: letters to your baby, gratitude lines and daily moods; and the baby names you save.

All of this is kept in one file in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. The app does not send it anywhere and does not sync it to iCloud. We cannot see it. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), the backup includes this file, as it includes other apps' data; backups are under your control through your Apple Account.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to confirm and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID that the app creates, your App Store purchase history for this app (which products, when, and whether a subscription is active) and technical details needed to process it, such as the app version and the App Store's country and currency. None of the details you enter in the app are sent, and the anonymous ID is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account by us. We use this information only to give you what you bought.

Voices, reminders and links

  • Voices: chanting, stories and meditations are read by your iPhone's own speech voices, on the device.
  • Reminders: every reminder (the daily one, your week and month, festivals, milestones and appointments) is worked out and scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server. Appointment reminders show the name you gave the appointment and its time, never your notes. If you choose to keep reminder text private, every reminder says only "Your sadhana is ready". A line you write from a reminder is saved in the app's journal on your iPhone.
  • Widgets: to show this week's shloka on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, the app writes a small file (your week and month, and the shloka) to an area on your iPhone that only the app and its widget can read. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Sharing: when you share a shloka or a line for your partner, you choose where it goes; the picture holds only the verse, its meaning and the app's name.
  • Links: the privacy, terms and help links open in your browser, where those sites' own policies apply.

Health information

Your due date, contraction and movement notes, appointments and moods are health-related. They stay on your iPhone as described above. We don't receive them, sell them, share them or use them for advertising or research.

Children

The app is for adults who are expecting or have just had a baby. It is not directed at children, and it collects no information about anyone for us.

Your choices

  • Change or remove any detail in the app's Settings.
  • Erase everything at once with Settings → Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to give you or delete. RevenueCat keeps anonymous purchase records for as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal requirements. Wherever you live, if you have a question about your rights or this policy, please contact us and we will help.

Changes

If the app ever handles information differently, we will update this page and its date before the change reaches you.
