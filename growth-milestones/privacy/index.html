PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026
The short version Your family archive stays on your device. Optional, content-free app analytics are off by default and start only if you choose to help improve Growth Milestones. There is no account, advertising, social profile, or paywall.

1. Who we are

Growth Milestones is developed and published by Bairat Labs, LLC ("Bairat Labs," "we," "us"). This policy explains how the Growth Milestones mobile app handles information on iOS and Android.

2. Your family archive stays local

Growth Milestones does not upload your family archive to Bairat Labs or Firebase Analytics. We never receive, view, or store any of the following:

  • Your child's name, birthdate, or profile photo
  • Height, weight, or other growth measurements
  • Milestone notes, dates, journal entries, or custom memories
  • Photos you attach to a memory or child profile
  • Account credentials, because no account is ever created

This information is generated by you, stored only on your device, and never leaves it unless you explicitly export or share it yourself, such as by exporting a PDF memory book or saving a backup file.

3. How information is stored on your device

Child profiles, milestones, journal entries, growth measurements, and settings are saved locally through React Native AsyncStorage. Photos you select are copied into the app's private document directory. None of this family content is synced to a cloud service, and Bairat Labs has no access to it.

4. Backup, export, and the memory book PDF

Growth Milestones lets you generate a few types of files entirely on your device:

  • JSON backup/export - a file containing your child profiles, milestones, journal entries, growth measurements, and settings. This file is created and read locally; Bairat Labs never receives a copy.
  • Memory book PDF - a printable keepsake document generated on your device from your timeline, photos, and growth charts.

Because these exports are created locally, what you do with the resulting file (where you save it, who you share it with) is entirely up to you. We recommend storing backups somewhere only you control.

5. Photo library access

If you attach a photo to a child profile or memory, the app asks for permission to access your photo library. Selected photos are copied into the app's private local storage and are never uploaded by the app. Growth Milestones does not request camera, location, microphone, contacts, calendar, health, notification, or background-execution permissions.

6. Optional app analytics

After setup, Growth Milestones asks whether you want to share limited feature-use and basic session events with Bairat Labs through Google Analytics for Firebase. This setting is off unless you allow it. Declining does not limit any feature, and you can change the choice in Settings.

If you choose Share app usage, the app may send:

  • Fixed, content-free events, such as whether a memory was created, search or filters were used, a resurfaced memory was opened, or an export completed
  • A photo count when photos are attached, never the photos or filenames
  • Basic app lifecycle and session events generated by Firebase Analytics
  • A random app-instance identifier used to distinguish one app installation from another
  • Basic app and device information, language, operating-system version, and approximate country or region derived from a masked IP address
  • Limited Firebase installation and SDK diagnostic data used to operate analytics

Analytics events never include a child's name or identifier, birthdate, milestone or journal text, notes, photos, filenames, measurements, memory dates, search words, contact details, or backup contents. We do not set a user ID or custom user properties.

Advertising identifiers, advertising use, automatic screen reporting, and ad-network registration are disabled. Growth Milestones does not use analytics data for advertising, cross-app tracking, personalized marketing, or a social profile. Google processes the optional analytics as our service provider, and data is encrypted in transit.

If you turn analytics off, the app stops future collection and resets this installation's analytics identifier. Previously received aggregate reports cannot be located or deleted through the app because Growth Milestones has no account or Bairat Labs user identifier.

7. Children's privacy

Growth Milestones is installed and used by a parent or legal guardian to record information about their own child. The app is not intended for direct use by a child. Family archive content is not sent to Bairat Labs or Firebase Analytics. Optional analytics describe the parent's use of app features and do not contain child profile or memory content.

8. Your rights and choices

You can access, edit, or delete family records directly in the app; create a portable backup from Settings; or uninstall the app to remove its local sandbox. You can decline optional analytics, turn Share app usage off at any time, and separately review or erase the private product-insight counters stored only on your device.

Because Growth Milestones has no account and sends no user ID, Bairat Labs cannot identify an individual in Firebase Analytics or retrieve an individual's family archive.

9. Security

Your family archive is protected primarily by your device's application sandbox and device security, including its passcode, biometric lock, and encryption. Optional analytics data is encrypted in transit. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date and using a passcode or biometric lock.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this same address.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how Growth Milestones handles information? Contact Bairat Labs, LLC at bairatlabs@gmail.com.
