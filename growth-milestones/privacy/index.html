PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026
The short version Growth Milestones is a private baby book and growth tracker. It has no account, analytics, advertising, or server. Child profiles, photos, milestones, growth measurements, journal entries, and settings stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Growth Milestones is developed and published by Bairat Labs, LLC ("Bairat Labs," "we," "us"). This policy explains how the Growth Milestones mobile app handles information on iOS and Android.

2. No data collection

Growth Milestones does not have a backend server. The app makes no network requests to Bairat Labs, and we never receive, view, or store any of the following:

  • Your child's name, birthdate, or profile photo
  • Height, weight, or other growth measurements
  • Milestone notes, dates, journal entries, or custom memories
  • Photos you attach to a memory or child profile
  • Account credentials, because no account is ever created

All of this information is generated by you, stored only on your device, and never leaves it unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (for example, by exporting a PDF memory book or saving a backup file).

3. How information is stored on your device

Child profiles, milestones, journal entries, growth measurements, and settings are saved locally through React Native AsyncStorage. Photos you select are copied into the app's private document directory. None of this data is synced to a cloud service, and Bairat Labs has no access to it.

4. Backup, export, and the memory book PDF

Growth Milestones lets you generate a few types of files entirely on your device:

  • JSON backup/export - a file containing your child profiles, milestones, journal entries, growth measurements, and settings. This file is created and read locally; Bairat Labs never receives a copy.
  • Memory book PDF - a printable keepsake document generated on your device from your timeline, photos, and growth charts.

Because these exports are created locally, what you do with the resulting file (where you save it, who you share it with) is entirely up to you. We recommend storing backups somewhere only you control.

5. Photo library access

If you attach a photo to a child profile or memory, the app asks for permission to access your photo library. Selected photos are copied into the app's private local storage and are never uploaded by the app. Growth Milestones does not request camera, location, microphone, contacts, calendar, health, notification, or background-execution permissions.

6. No third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics

Growth Milestones does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service that transmits personal data off your device. There are no third-party trackers embedded in the app.

7. Children's privacy

Growth Milestones is installed and used by a parent or legal guardian to record information about their own child. The app is not intended for direct use by a child. Because this information is stored exclusively on the parent's device and is never transmitted to or collected by Bairat Labs, we have no ability to identify any individual child or family.

8. Your rights and choices

Because all data lives on your device, you control it directly. You can access memories in the app, export a backup from Settings, edit or delete individual memories and profiles, or uninstall the app to remove its local data. We cannot fulfil access, deletion, or portability requests on your behalf because we do not hold your data.

9. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, the main safeguard is your device's own security (passcode, biometric lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a passcode or biometric lock to protect access to the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this same address.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how Growth Milestones handles information? Contact Bairat Labs, LLC at bairatlabs@gmail.com.
