Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

This policy explains how Bairat Labs, LLC ("Bairat Labs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Bairat Studio.

Information we process

When you connect a social account, the platform provides an account identifier, display information, authorization tokens, granted permissions, and token expiry information. We also process content you choose to prepare or publish, including media, captions, destination accounts, scheduling instructions, and publishing status.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate connected accounts, show account and publishing settings, deliver approved content to the selected platform, monitor publishing status, prevent duplicate publishing, secure the service, and provide support.

Platform access

Bairat Studio uses official authorization and publishing interfaces provided by connected platforms. We do not ask for or store your TikTok or Instagram password. You can revoke a platform connection through the platform or Bairat Studio.

Sharing

We send content and required account data to the platform you select so it can complete the requested action. We may use infrastructure providers to host the service and store encrypted credentials. We do not sell personal information or social account data.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained while an account remains connected. Publishing records are retained as needed to show status, prevent duplicates, and investigate failures. You may request disconnection and deletion by emailing us. We may retain limited security or legal records when required.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, restricted secret storage, and least-privilege permissions. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may disconnect an account, revoke permissions through the connected platform, or ask us to delete associated Bairat Studio data. Revoking access stops new publishing actions but does not remove content already published by a platform.
