Data deletion

Delete your data

Delete an account and synchronized data in the app

  1. Open Picky Eating Reset.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Everything under Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion.

This removes the parent account and current synchronized child profile, food history, progress, preferences, and account identifier. It also clears the app's local data on that device.

Delete local data without an account

Use the same Delete Everything control in Settings. If no parent account is active, this clears the child profile, accepted foods, preparation details, exposure history, ladders, progress, preferences, and coach questions stored by the app on that device.

Get deletion help

If you cannot access the app, email bairatlabs@gmail.com from the address associated with the account. We may ask you to verify control of the account before deletion.

Operational logs are retained for 30 days. Limited transaction, security, or legal records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or transaction reconciliation.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through the Apple App Store or Google Play account used for purchase.
