Data deletion

Delete your data

Delete local app data

  1. Open Picky Eating Reset.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all local data.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

This removes the child profile, accepted foods, preparation details, exposure history, ladders, progress, and preferences stored by the app on that device.

Delete an account and cloud data

Email bairatlabs@gmail.com from the address associated with the account and use the subject “Delete My Picky Eating Reset Account.” We may ask you to verify control of the account before deletion.

Account deletion includes synced profiles, exposure history, coach history, and account identifiers, except records that must be retained for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or transaction reconciliation.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through the Apple App Store or Google Play account used for purchase.
