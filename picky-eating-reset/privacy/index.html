Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

The short version
Picky Eating Reset is for parents. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or create independent child accounts. Profile information is used to personalize food-exploration guidance and apply safety filters.

1. Who we are

Picky Eating Reset is developed and published by Bairat Labs, LLC, a United States software company. Contact us at bairatlabs@gmail.com or at 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713, United States.

2. Information you provide

A parent may provide an email address, a child first name or nickname, age band, accepted foods, preparation details, sensory preferences, allergies, dietary exclusions, food-exposure outcomes, optional notes, and coach questions. Do not enter medical records.

3. How information is used

  • Build and personalize the child's Food Profile and daily plan.
  • Apply allergy and dietary exclusions as recommendation filters.
  • Save progress and synchronize it across devices when an account is enabled.
  • Generate a requested coach response.
  • Manage subscription entitlement and restore purchases.
  • Provide support, security, and service reliability.

4. On-device storage

The app stores the Food Profile, plan progress, preferences, and exposure history on the device so core information remains available. Authentication tokens are stored using protected device storage. Local data can be removed from Settings.

5. Service providers

Production services may use Amazon Web Services for authentication, API processing, encrypted application storage, and operational logs; RevenueCat for subscription status; Apple and Google for store purchases; and a disclosed AI provider for coach responses. Payment-card details are handled by Apple or Google and are not received by Bairat Labs.

6. AI coach

Coach questions and the minimum relevant profile context are transmitted only when a parent submits a question. The coach is not an emergency, diagnostic, or treatment service. Provider and retention settings are configured to prevent use of API content for advertising or model training where supported.

7. Children

The app is installed, controlled, and used by a parent or legal guardian. Child Explorer is a brief shared activity. It does not create a child account or request a child's email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

8. Retention and deletion

Local information remains until the parent deletes it or removes the app. Account information is retained while the account is active and for a limited period needed for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and backups. Parents can delete local data in Settings and request account deletion at our deletion page.

9. Security and international processing

We use encrypted transport, access controls, and provider security features designed to protect information. No system is perfectly secure. Bairat Labs is based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States.

10. Your choices

You can decline account sync, disable notifications, edit profile details, restore purchases, delete local data, or contact us to request access, correction, portability, or deletion where applicable.

11. Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, providing notice in the app. Privacy questions can be sent to bairatlabs@gmail.com.
