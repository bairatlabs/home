Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

The short version
Picky Eating Reset is for parents. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, use cross-app tracking, or create independent child accounts. Profile information is used to personalize food-exploration guidance and apply safety filters.

1. Who we are

Picky Eating Reset is developed and published by Bairat Labs, LLC, a United States software company. Contact us at bairatlabs@gmail.com or at 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713, United States.

2. Information you provide

A parent may provide an email address, a child first name or nickname, age band, accepted foods, preparation details, sensory preferences, allergies, dietary exclusions, food-exposure outcomes, optional notes, and coach questions. Do not enter medical records. Coach questions are processed on the device and are not sent to Bairat Labs or an AI provider.

3. How information is used

  • Build and personalize the child's Food Profile and daily plan.
  • Apply allergy and dietary exclusions as recommendation filters.
  • Save progress and synchronize it across devices when a parent enables an account.
  • Generate deterministic coach guidance on the device.
  • Manage subscription entitlement and restore purchases.
  • Provide support, security, and service reliability.

4. On-device storage

The app stores the Food Profile, plan progress, preferences, exposure history, and coach questions on the device so core information remains available. Authentication tokens use protected device storage. Local data can be removed in Settings.

5. Account sync and service providers

If a parent enables an account, profile and progress information is synchronized through Amazon Web Services, which provides email authentication, API processing, encrypted application storage, and operational logs. RevenueCat provides subscription-status services. Apple and Google process store purchases. Payment-card details are handled by Apple or Google and are not received by Bairat Labs.

6. On-device coach

The coach uses deterministic guidance included in the app. Coach questions and profile context are not transmitted to Bairat Labs or a third-party AI provider. The coach is not an emergency, diagnostic, or treatment service.

7. Children

The app is installed, controlled, and used by a parent or legal guardian. Child Explorer is a brief shared activity. It does not create a child account or request a child's email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

8. Retention and deletion

Local information remains until the parent deletes it or removes the app. Account information remains while the account is active. A parent can permanently delete the account and current synchronized app record in Settings. Operational logs are retained for 30 days. Limited transaction, security, or legal records may be retained where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or transaction reconciliation. See Delete your data for instructions.

9. Security and international processing

We use encrypted transport, access controls, and provider security features designed to protect information. No system is perfectly secure. Bairat Labs and the production app infrastructure are based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States.

10. Your choices

You can decline account sync, disable notifications, edit profile details, restore purchases, delete local data, delete your account and synchronized data in Settings, or contact us to request access, correction, portability, or deletion where applicable.

11. Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, providing notice in the app. Privacy questions can be sent to bairatlabs@gmail.com.
