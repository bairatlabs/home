Safety concern

Difficulty swallowing, choking, pain, significant vomiting, growth concerns, sudden major food reduction, or serious allergy concerns require an appropriate clinician.

Emergency
If a child is choking or has symptoms of a severe allergic reaction, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for an app response.
