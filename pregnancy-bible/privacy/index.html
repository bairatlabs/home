Pregnancy Bible: Read to Baby

Privacy Policy: Pregnancy Bible

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Pregnancy Bible: Read to Baby ("the app") is made by Bairat Labs, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the app on iPhone and Android, our servers that it talks to (api.bairatlabs.com and cdn.bairatlabs.com), family invite links (bairatlabs.com/i/…), and the app's pages on bairatlabs.com.

The short version

  • What you enter stays on your phone: your plan, readers, due date, baby's name, readings, progress and recordings. Your due date, your baby's name and your baby's arrival never reach us.
  • To give you what you bought, we keep a record of your purchase and a random ID for your installation.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email address and sign-in details, and your phone registers with us so that family nudges can reach it.
  • Your settings and readings reach us only while you're in a family, and your recordings only if you turn on backup.
  • No ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data. RevenueCat gives us reports on purchases, for our own use only.
  • You can delete your data from the app (Settings, then Delete my data), or by writing to us.

Who the app is for

The app is made for adults: an expectant mother and her family. You must be 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live, if that is higher) to buy in the app or to create an account. The app isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Someone reading aloud on your phone may be a child. Their name stays on your phone, and so does their voice, unless you turn on recording backup, which then sends their recordings to us. Turn it on only if you're their parent or have their parent's permission. You can delete those copies at any time. A parent who isn't the account holder can write to us with the account's email address, and we will work with the account holder to delete their child's recordings.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in the app stays on your phone: your plan and settings, your readers' names, your due date, your baby's name, whether and when your baby has arrived, your readings and progress, and your recordings. Your daily reminders are scheduled on your phone, and may name your baby. Your week of pregnancy is worked out on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Your phone's own backup. Your phone's backup can include the app's data on your phone: its database (your plan, readers, progress, the list of recordings and, if you're signed in, your account's ID, sign-in method and email address) and your recordings. Your due date and your baby's name are never included: they stay on that phone only, so on a new phone the app asks for them again. On iPhone, that is your iCloud or computer backup. On Android, they can move to a new phone with a phone-to-phone transfer, and go into your Google backup while the app's data is small: Google backs up at most 25 MB for each app, about half an hour of recordings, so don't count on Google's backup for your recordings. On Android, the backup also includes RevenueCat's record of your purchases on that phone. Android backs them up only when it can encrypt them with your screen lock. Those copies are in your Apple or Google account, under your settings, and we can't read or delete them. Sign-in tokens are never included: they stay in your phone's keychain or keystore, on that phone only.

What we collect, and why

When you buy

  • Purchases. You buy through the App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat checks the purchase for us. We keep the store, the product, the transaction ID and the purchase date, with RevenueCat's ID for you (an anonymous ID until you sign in). We use them to give you access, to restore it, and to handle refunds. RevenueCat also uses your RevenueCat ID and purchases to give us purchase reports (how many people buy, and where), which we use only to run the app. Neither we nor RevenueCat ever see your payment card.
  • An installation ID. The app makes a random ID for your installation. It sends it with each purchase check, and when you sign in. We use it to give access, and to notice a purchase used on more than 10 phones in 30 days, which we flag for review (our Terms of Use, section 8, say when we may suspend access). We keep the record that a purchase was used on an installation until 30 days after that installation's last purchase check. While you're signed in, the same ID also identifies your phone's registration for nudges (below).
  • What RevenueCat receives. RevenueCat's software in the app also sends RevenueCat your device model, system and app version, preferred languages, store country, and on iPhone Apple's identifier for apps from the same developer. RevenueCat keeps a country worked out from your IP address and the last time the app was opened; it doesn't keep the IP address.

When you sign in (optional)

You never need to sign in to buy or to read. Signing in keeps your purchase with your account, so you can use it on iPhone and Android alike, and you need it for Family and for backing up recordings.

  • Sign in with Apple (on iPhone): Apple's ID for you, and your email address or Apple's private relay address. We don't ask Apple for your name. We keep Apple's refresh token, encrypted, only so that we can revoke Sign in with Apple when you delete your account.
  • Google (on iPhone): Google's ID for you and your email address. Google's sign-in also carries your name and photo, which we don't keep.
  • Email code (on iPhone and Android): your email address. We send you a 6-digit code through ZeptoMail.
  • We don't keep Apple's or Google's identity tokens. Signing in also sends your installation ID and whether you're on iPhone or Android. Your account ID becomes your ID at RevenueCat, so your purchase follows your account.

When you're signed in

  • Your phone's registration for nudges. So that a family nudge can reach you, each signed-in phone registers with us, whether or not you're in a family: its installation ID, its push token (none while notifications are off), iPhone or Android (and on iPhone, the push environment), the app version, and which of our apps it is. The app renews the registration each day you use it. Signing out removes it, and one that isn't renewed lapses after 30 days. On Android, Google's Firebase also makes an installation ID for the app once you sign in, which Google receives and we don't.
  • While you're in a family, what the family needs from your phone is kept with your account, so everyone sees the same day: the display name you choose (which your family sees), your time zone, translation, minutes a day and plan, and your readings: your progress on each track with when you started and finished it, a dated log of each day's reading with how long it took (for every reader on your phone, by an ID rather than a name), and your leaps. Your readers' names, reminder time and appearance stay on your phone. None of this reaches us before you join a family.
  • After you leave a family, are removed from it, or the family ends, what was kept while you were in it stays with your account until you delete your account (Settings, then Delete my data).
  • Your due date, your baby's name, and whether and when your baby has arrived stay on your phone, even while you're signed in or in a family. The app doesn't send them to us. If we ever offer to keep them with your account, it will be off unless you turn it on, and we will update this policy first.

Family

When you buy Pregnancy Bible for Family, you can invite up to three people. Each needs their own sign-in.

  • We keep each member's display name and relation (partner, grandparent or other), who has read today, and totals of sessions read, time spent reading and leaps. We also keep the family's invite links, with the account ID of whoever accepted each one.
  • Everyone in the family sees each member's display name and relation, who has read today, and the totals. Members' apps also receive the owner's translation, minutes a day and current reading, but don't show them: each member keeps their own plan. Members never see your recordings, your due date or your baby's name.
  • Anyone who opens an invite link sees the display name of the person who invited them before signing in. Each link works once and expires after 7 days.
  • Nudges. The family's owner can send a member one gentle reminder a day. It goes through Apple's or Google's push service to the member's registered phone, and carries the member's display name, never your baby's name.
  • If you leave someone else's family, or delete your account, that family's invite history keeps the account ID you joined with (a random ID, not your name or email address) until that family ends.

Recording backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, your recordings and their details (the reading, its length and date, and which reader, by an ID rather than a name) are stored in our private storage, encrypted, with your account. The app doesn't copy them back onto a phone; if you need a copy, write to us. We never listen to them, and we never share them, with your family or anyone else. You can delete one copy or all of them in Settings. When you turn backup off, you choose whether the copies already stored stay with us or are deleted.

When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you. Our support inbox is a Gmail account, so Google holds those emails under its own terms (see "Who receives data").

What we don't collect

  • No precise location, and nothing from your phone's location services. While you're in a family we keep your time zone, and RevenueCat keeps a country (see "When you buy").
  • No contacts, photos or browsing history.
  • No advertising or crash-reporting software, no analytics software beyond RevenueCat's purchase reports, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Our logs never record receipts or pregnancy details, and we don't write tokens, sign-in codes or the content of emails to them. Our firewall keeps no samples of requests.

Deleting your data, and your other choices

  • In the app: Settings, then Delete my data. If you're signed in, this deletes your account and what we hold for it (settings, readings, your family, backed-up recordings and purchase records), asks RevenueCat to delete your customer record there and Apple to revoke Sign in with Apple, and then erases the app's data on your phone. The deletion on our servers runs in the background; your sign-in stops working at once. If RevenueCat or Apple can't be reached after three tries, the rest of the deletion still completes; write to us if you want us to check that step. If you're signed out, Delete my data erases the app's data on your phone only.
  • What stays on your phone afterwards: a random installation ID, the downloaded readings (Scripture only), and RevenueCat's record on that phone of your purchases (with your anonymous RevenueCat ID if you never signed in). Deleting the app removes them, though on iPhone the installation ID can survive reinstalling the app.
  • Some of it: delete one backed-up recording or all of them, turn off backup (keeping or deleting the copies already stored), or remove your email address or a sign-in method. Leaving a family takes you out of it, but what was kept while you were in it stays with your account until you delete your account.
  • Without the app: see Delete your data, or write to us at bairatlabs@gmail.com.
  • If you bought without signing in, we hold your purchase record and the installations it was checked on, under an anonymous ID. Write to us with the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt, and we will find them and delete them, and ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record there.
  • If you started an email sign-up and never entered the code, write to us from that address and we will delete it.
  • Your purchase belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so you can restore it after deleting your data.
  • Copies: database backups age out within 35 days, and the record that a purchase was used on an installation within 30 days.
  • Seeing or correcting your data: write to us. We answer within one month. If we need longer, where the law allows it, we tell you why within that month. Depending on where you live, the law may give you the right to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and to appeal if we refuse. We honour these requests from everyone, wherever they live.

Your privacy rights in US states

Some US state laws, such as California's and Delaware's, describe your rights in their own terms. In those terms:

  • What we collect: identifiers (your email address, account ID, installation ID and push token), commercial information (your purchase record), audio (recordings you back up), internet activity (our server logs and firewall samples), and sensitive information, because using a pregnancy app or a Bible app can reveal something about your health or your religious beliefs. Where it comes from, why we use it and who receives it are set out above.
  • No selling or sharing. We don't sell personal information, or share it for advertising across other companies' apps or websites, and we haven't in the past 12 months.
  • Sensitive information is used only to provide the app you asked for, never to work out things about you.
  • No discrimination. Using your rights never changes the price you pay or the app you get.
  • Someone acting for you. You can have someone else make a request for you, with your signed permission. We may check it with you.
  • How we confirm it's you: write from the email address on your account, or include the order number from your store receipt.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so there is nothing for these signals to turn off. We treat them as on for everyone.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Our database and backed-up recordings are encrypted with our own encryption key, and our logs with keys managed by AWS. Backed-up recordings are in private storage, and anything removed from it is really deleted. Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in its keychain or keystore.

Where data is processed

In the United States (AWS, RevenueCat and GitHub), in India for sign-in code emails (Zoho), and worldwide where Apple, Google and Cloudflare run their services.

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, we use your data to provide the app and what you buy (our contract with you), to keep the service secure and stop misuse, such as a purchase shared across many phones (our legitimate interests), and to meet our legal duties. Backup and Family are things you choose, and you can stop them at any time. You never have to give us data to read: buying needs your purchase record, and signing in, Family and backup need what they use. We make no decisions about you by automated means alone. Your data goes to the United States and India, as set out above. You can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to us, and we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Consumer health data (Washington and similar laws)

Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws, such as Nevada's, give you rights over consumer health data. This is how we handle it.

What it is. Consumer health data is personal information that identifies your past, present or future health, including reproductive health, and what can be inferred about it from other data.

What we don't collect. Your due date, your baby's name, and whether and when your baby has arrived stay on your phone. The app doesn't send them to us.

What we collect, and why. Because the app is for pregnancy, anything we hold that is linked to you could show that you are, or were, expecting a baby. So we treat all of it as consumer health data:

Who receives it.

Your rights. You can:

  • confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, see it, and get a list of everyone we have shared it with or sold it to (we sell it to no one);
  • have it corrected: write to us;
  • withdraw any consent you have given us;
  • have it deleted: Delete my data in the app, or write to us. We delete it, and tell our service providers to, within 45 days (we may extend this once by 45 days, and will tell you why). If you never signed in, Delete my data erases your phone only: to delete your purchase record and your record at RevenueCat, write to us with the order number from your store receipt.

What is kept after deletion, and why. Database backups age out within 35 days. A few records outlast that, to keep the service secure and running: the record that a purchase was used on an installation (30 days after its last purchase check, under an anonymous ID), server logs (90 days, with your account ID, and rarely your email address), ZeptoMail's delivery logs (60 days, with your email address), and our audit trail of calls to AWS services (6 months, with your account ID, and your email address if the account was created or its email changed). An email sign-up you never confirmed stays until you ask us to delete it.

How to use them, and to appeal. Write to bairatlabs@gmail.com with "Consumer health data" in the subject, from the address on your account or with your store receipt so that we can confirm it's you. If we refuse a request, we tell you why, and you can appeal by replying to that message within 45 days. We answer an appeal in writing within 45 days, and if we refuse it, we tell you how to contact the Washington State Attorney General.
