Pregnancy Bible: Read to Baby

Delete your Pregnancy Bible data

Updated 15 October 2026

You can delete your data in the app, or ask us by email. This page is for Pregnancy Bible: Read to Baby, made by Bairat Labs, LLC.

In the app

  1. Open Pregnancy Bible.
  2. On Today, tap Settings, the button at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my data.
  4. Read what will be deleted, then tap Delete my data to confirm.

If you're signed in, this deletes your account and what we hold for it, then erases the app's data on your phone. If you're signed out, it erases the app's data on your phone only. To delete your account and anything kept with it as well, sign in first.

Delete my data erases the phone you use it on. If Pregnancy Bible is on another phone too, use Delete my data there as well.

By email

Write to bairatlabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pregnancy Bible data".

Email a deletion request

  • Send it from the email address you sign in with.
  • If you never signed in, or signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt, so that we can find your purchase record.
  • Please don't send us a sign-in code.

We may reply to check that it's you. We delete your data, and tell our processors to, within 45 days.

If you never signed in, we hold your purchase record and the installations it was checked on, under an anonymous ID. With your order number we find them and delete them, and ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record there. If you started signing in with your email and never entered the code, write from that address and we delete it too.

What is deleted, and when

Your sign-in stops working the moment you confirm, on every phone. The app then erases its data on your phone, and the rest is deleted on our servers in the background:

  • your account, and your ways to sign in (Apple, Google or email). We also ask Apple to revoke Sign in with Apple for the app;
  • your settings, progress and readings kept with your account while you were in a family, including a family you have left since. Your due date and baby's name never reach us, so there is nothing of them to delete on our servers;
  • your family. If you own it, it ends: your family members lose the access your purchase gave them, and open invites stop working. If you're a member, you leave it;
  • recordings you backed up with us;
  • your purchase records with us. We also ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record there;
  • your phones' registrations for family nudges;
  • on your phone: your plan, readers, due date, baby's name, progress, recordings and reminders.

If Apple or RevenueCat can't be reached after three tries, the rest of the deletion still completes. Write to us if you want us to check that step.

Copies in your own phone backup

Your iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, or your Google backup on Android (while the app's data is under Google's 25 MB limit for each app), can include the app's data on your phone, and your recordings. Those copies are yours, in your Apple or Google account and under your settings. We can't read or delete them, and Delete my data doesn't touch them. You can manage them in your phone's backup settings.

Nothing to cancel

Pregnancy Bible is paid once, with no subscription, so there's nothing to cancel. Deleting your data doesn't refund a purchase: refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Your purchase stays with your store account, and you can restore it any time.

Delete some of it

In the app you can also delete one backed-up recording or all of them, turn off backup (you choose whether the copies already stored are kept or deleted), or remove your email address or a sign-in method. Leaving a family doesn't delete what was kept with your account while you were in it: that goes when you delete your account.
