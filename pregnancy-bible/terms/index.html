1. What the app is

The app gives you a short Bible passage to read aloud to your baby each day, lets you record your voice as you read, and keeps your recordings as a keepsake.

It is a faith and bonding app, not a medical app. It doesn't track symptoms, give health advice, or replace care from a doctor or midwife. The week of pregnancy it shows is worked out from the due date you enter. If you have any concern about your health or your baby's, talk to a medical professional.

You must be 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live, if that is higher) to buy in the app or to create an account. Others, such as family members or a child, may read aloud on your phone with you.

5. Family

  • With Pregnancy Bible for Family, you (the owner) can invite up to three family members at a time, each on their own phone with their own sign-in. Over the life of the purchase, up to six different people can be members.
  • Invite links work once and expire after 7 days. Share them only with people you mean to invite.
  • Everyone in the family sees who has read today and everyone's totals. Each member keeps their own reading plan. Members never see your recordings, your due date or your baby's name. Each member's recordings stay on their own phone.
  • You can remove a member, and a member can leave, at any time.
  • The family ends when you delete your account, and members then lose the access your purchase gave them. If Pregnancy Bible for Family or Add family is refunded (section 3), members lose that access, but the family itself stays until you remove its members or delete your account.
  • As the owner, you can send each member one gentle nudge a day. Use it kindly.
