Pregnancy Bible: Read to Baby

From about week 20 your baby can hear you. Pregnancy Bible gives you a short Bible passage to read aloud to your baby each evening, in five, ten or fifteen minutes.

Support

Write to us at bairatlabs@gmail.com with "Pregnancy Bible" in the subject. Tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what happened. Please don't send us a sign-in code.

Email bairatlabs@gmail.com

Restoring your purchase

Your purchase belongs to your App Store or Google Play account. On the purchase sheet, tap Restore purchase, or on the first screen, tap "I've already bought it".

Paid once

There's no subscription, so there's nothing to cancel. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Delete my data. You can also ask us by email: see Delete your data.

Privacy and terms

  • Privacy Policy: what stays on your phone, what reaches us and why, and your choices.
  • Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy: how we handle consumer health data, under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws.
  • Terms of Use: purchases, accounts, Family and fair use.
  • Delete your data: in the app or by email, and what is deleted and kept.

About

Pregnancy Bible is a faith and bonding app, made by Bairat Labs, LLC. It doesn't track symptoms or give health advice.

Scripture: Berean Standard Bible (public domain) and King James Version (public domain outside the UK). Bible text compiled from scrollmapper/bible_databases (MIT).
