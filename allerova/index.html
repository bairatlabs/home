Check ingredient labels

Photograph a complete packaged-food ingredient list. Allerova reads it on your device and compares the text with known allergies saved in a local profile.

Evidence, not a safety claim

Allerova reports what it found, where it found it, and when there is not enough information. It never labels a food safe. Always check the original package. Allerova does not diagnose allergies or replace medical advice.

Private by design

No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, subscription, or cloud upload. Your profile and scan history stay in local app storage.
