Help with Allerova

Before every check

Photograph or paste the complete ingredient list, including any Contains or May contain statement. Make sure the text is sharp and the list is not cut off.

Camera access

If camera access is off, enable it in your device settings or use Paste ingredients in Allerova.

Understanding results

No listed conflict found is not a guarantee that a food is allergen-free. OCR can misread text, labels can be incomplete, and recipes can change. Check the original package every time.

Subscriptions

The free plan includes three label checks each week. Allerova Pro adds unlimited checks and full scan history. Use Restore purchases in Allerova Settings if you reinstall the app or move to another device while using the same Apple Account. Use the subscription row in Settings to manage or cancel an active subscription.

Contact

Email bairatlabs@gmail.com and include your device type, app version, and a description of the problem. Do not email medical records or other sensitive information.
