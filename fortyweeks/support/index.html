Help and support

Updated October 13, 2026

Questions about FortyWeeks, or something not working? Email bairatlabs@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How do I change my due date?

Settings › Due date. Your plan and every week’s rules move with it.

My baby is here. What now?

Settings › My baby is here. Enter the birth date and how you gave birth, and the app switches to your postpartum return: breathing and gentle reconnection first, then strength after your postnatal check.

Why is a session locked?

Some conditions mean your provider should clear you before most exercise. Those sessions open when you confirm your provider has cleared you. After birth, strength training waits for your postnatal check.

What if something feels wrong during a session?

Stop. Open the stop card from the session screen; if you notice any sign on it, contact your provider.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete my data. Everything is stored only on your phone, so that, or deleting the app, removes it all.

Is this medical advice?

No. FortyWeeks gives general exercise guidance. Check with your provider before you start or change exercise.
