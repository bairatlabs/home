FortyWeeks

Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026

FortyWeeks is made by Bairat Labs, LLC (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: what you tell FortyWeeks stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app connects to the internet only for the one-time purchase, which RevenueCat checks for us under a random ID, never your name.

What the app keeps, and where

To build your plan, the app asks for your due date (or the first day of your last period, or an IVF transfer date), or, after birth, your baby’s birth date and how you gave birth. It also asks how active you are, where you train, how long you usually have, and any conditions your provider has mentioned.

Each day you can log how you feel: energy, nausea, pain, swelling and similar. The app also keeps which sessions you did and how they felt, your answers to the postpartum checks, and your daily practice streak.

All of this is stored only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we can’t see it.

RevenueCat’s software in the app (see below) also keeps, on your phone, the random ID it uses for the purchase and a record of what you bought, so the app knows what is yours when you’re offline.

On Android, once you have bought, RevenueCat’s software also saves that random ID with Google’s Block Store, which can keep it in your Google account’s backup, so a reinstall or a new phone finds your purchase. Like your phone’s backup, it belongs to your Google account, it may outlast deleting the app, and we have no access to it.

Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or your Google backup) may include the app’s storage. That backup belongs to your Apple or Google account and their terms apply to it. We have no access to it.

What leaves your phone

Nothing you enter. Your plan, your answers, your check-ins, your session history and anything about your health never leave your phone. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The app connects to the internet only for the one-time purchase: to show the store’s price, to buy, and to check and restore what you bought. You buy through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat, Inc., a company in the United States, checks purchases for us as our service provider.

Each time the app checks for a purchase or loads the price, which it does when it opens, RevenueCat receives a random ID that its software makes for the app on your phone (not your name, your email address or any account), your store’s country, your device model, system and app version, and your preferred languages. On iPhone it also receives Apple’s identifier for apps from the same developer. When you buy or restore, or the store tells the app about a purchase, RevenueCat also receives the store’s record of it: the transaction, the product, its price and currency, and the date. Like any internet service, RevenueCat sees the IP address the app connects from.

Neither we nor RevenueCat see your payment details: Apple or Google takes the payment, under their own privacy policies.

We use this only to give you what you bought, to restore it, to handle refunds and, through RevenueCat’s reports, to see how many people buy. We never use it for advertising, and we never sell it.

If you have chosen to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your phone’s settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous reports. These contain no information you entered in the app.

Health information

Information about your pregnancy, birth and symptoms is sensitive. It never leaves your phone: we don’t collect, sell or share it, and it is never used for advertising. The app uses it only to build your plan, on your phone.

A purchase record shows that FortyWeeks was bought on a store account, but it carries nothing you entered in the app.

How long it is kept

What you enter stays on your phone until you delete it (below). RevenueCat keeps the purchase records described above under its own privacy policy, at revenuecat.com/privacy, and we can have yours deleted at any time (below). Apple and Google keep your purchase with your store account under their policies.

Deleting your information

In the app, Settings › Delete my data removes your plan, check-ins and session history from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

RevenueCat files what it receives under the random ID, which Settings shows as your Purchase ID, and that ID is how we find your record. To have it deleted, copy the Purchase ID from Settings and email it to bairatlabs@gmail.com, before you delete the app. We will have RevenueCat delete the record. If you no longer have the app, write to us anyway with your store receipt, and we will try to find it from that.

Deleting the record doesn’t cancel or refund your purchase. The purchase belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so you can restore it afterwards with Restore purchase, which starts a new record at RevenueCat.

We hold none of your other information, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

Children

FortyWeeks is for adults. It isn’t directed to children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
