FortyWeeks

Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026

FortyWeeks is made by Bairat Labs, LLC (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: what you tell FortyWeeks stays on your phone. There are no accounts, and we don’t collect your data or track you.

What the app keeps, and where

To build your plan, the app asks for your due date (or the first day of your last period, or an IVF transfer date), or, after birth, your baby’s birth date and how you gave birth. It also asks how active you are, where you train, how long you usually have, and any conditions your provider has mentioned.

Each day you can log how you feel: energy, nausea, pain, swelling and similar. The app also keeps which sessions you did and how they felt, your answers to the postpartum checks, and your daily practice streak.

All of this is stored only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we can’t see it.

Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or your Google backup) may include the app’s storage. That backup belongs to your Apple or Google account and their terms apply to it. We have no access to it.

What leaves your phone

Nothing about you. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never sends your plan, answers or history anywhere. This version doesn’t connect to the internet at all.

Apple and Google handle the purchase of the app under their own privacy policies. If you have chosen to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your phone’s settings, they may send us anonymous reports. These contain no information you entered in the app.

Health information

Information about your pregnancy, birth and symptoms is sensitive. Because it never leaves your phone, we don’t collect, sell or share it, and it is never used for advertising. The app uses it only to build your plan, on your phone.

Deleting your information

In the app, Settings › Delete my data removes your plan, check-ins and session history from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. We hold none of your information, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

FortyWeeks is for adults. It isn’t directed to children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
