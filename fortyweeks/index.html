FortyWeeks

Pregnancy and postpartum workouts, week by week

FortyWeeks builds a strength, cardio, yoga and mobility plan for every week of pregnancy and the months after birth, with a five-minute pelvic floor practice every day. Every session follows your week, your symptoms and what your provider has said.

How your plan is made

Your plan comes from a program written by our trainer for every week of pregnancy and the first months after birth, based on the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists’ guidance on exercise in pregnancy and the postpartum period (Committee Opinion 804, 2020).

Its safety rules are built into the app: no lying flat on your back after week 20, no lying on your stomach from week 12, no jumping after the first trimester, a hand on support for balance work from week 21, no overhead pressing from week 28, and effort kept to a level where you can still talk. Symptoms you log change the day’s session, and some conditions lock sessions until your provider clears you.

After birth, the plan moves from breathing to pelvic floor and deep core work, then strength, then impact, at a pace set by your symptoms and your postnatal check.

FortyWeeks gives general exercise guidance. It is not medical advice: check with your provider before starting or changing exercise.
